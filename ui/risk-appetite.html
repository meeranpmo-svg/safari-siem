Risk Appetite (RAS)

The RAS monitoring sheet: sections, metrics and the six limits. Re-importing keeps every reported value and link. Limits that are out of order or on mixed scales are listed, never silently fixed.

Report many values

One line per value: metric,period,value,note (e.g. RAS-01.08,2026-10,262%,). All lines are checked first; if one is wrong nothing is recorded.
