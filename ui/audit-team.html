IT Audit Team with the AI IT Auditor

The AI IT Auditor works as a member of the audit team: it proposes the risk-based annual plan (every score is itemised with its source), draws samples from the platform's real records with a recorded seed so anyone can re-perform the draw, suggests the result of each attribute test, keeps the morning brief and answers questions from the audit records. People decide: auditors conclude every sample item and workpaper, the lead reviews and signs, the Head of Audit approves the plan, the final report and any risk acceptance - never the person who prepared it, never an AI identity. Audit records name people and weaknesses, so they are for operators and administrators only.
