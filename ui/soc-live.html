Press Start to stream events as the SIEM receives them.

Events appear here the moment the SIEM receives them - before they are stored - so this is the fastest view of what is happening. The Delay column is how long the event took to reach the SIEM: syslog devices are usually under a second; Windows servers depend on the agent's interval (every minute by default, every few seconds in real-time mode). At most 200 events per second are shown per viewer (the rest are counted as skipped) - narrow the filter for busy sources. Click a row for the full event. The last 1,000 rows are kept on screen. The analysis above runs in this browser over what this stream shows: it starts when you press Start and learns as it runs (a burst needs 2 minutes of history, a new pattern 2 minutes or 500 events); events skipped over 200 per second are not in it.
