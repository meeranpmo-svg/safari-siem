ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS

Clauses 4-10 and the 93 Annex A controls, checked from the platform's own evidence - the same checks and the same evidence collection as NCA ECC, so nothing is gathered twice and an NCA ECC control a person already confirmed (and a second person approved) counts here too. The internal audit programme is planned risk-first and each audit's working paper is prepared automatically a month before it is due; accepted findings become nonconformities and GRC actions; the management review pack and the Statement of Applicability are generated from the records. People decide every approval, audit and verification. Readiness is indicative - the certification body decides.
