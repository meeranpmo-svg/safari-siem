One-way Audio Troubleshooter

Who cannot hear whom? the call connects but the voice is lost one way - run it during or right after a bad call

Read-only: the voice sessions the firewalls send to the SIEM (both directions), the firewalls' own settings (SIP ALG, NAT, voice policies), WAN / tunnel events. Fixes are shown for a person to run, or proposed as a change plan that a different engineer approves - nothing is changed on any device from this page.

The diagnosis shows how the network is defended - analysts and above run it.
