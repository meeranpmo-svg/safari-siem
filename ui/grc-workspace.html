Risks & Actions

Where GRC is worked: every gap becomes a remediation action with an owner, a due date and evidence, closed only when someone other than whoever did the work verifies it; risks rated likelihood × impact (1–5) with treatment and acceptance by an administrator who is not the owner; policy exceptions that expire by date; and the evidence an auditor asks for, per control. Records come from your team, or from the platform itself with the record they came from (a SAMA gap, a failing control, an overdue drill, an asset without an owner) — nothing is estimated, and a risk is always rated by a person.
