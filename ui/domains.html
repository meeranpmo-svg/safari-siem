Domains & DNS

Every {{org.short}} internet domain and public hostname, checked from public DNS every 6 hours: renewal dates, nameservers, SPF / DMARC / DKIM (can someone send mail in our name?), CAA, and the TLS certificate on each hostname. A record that changed since the last check is listed, so an unapproved DNS change is visible. Read-only: nothing at a registrar or DNS provider is changed from here.
