HR Movers

Directory settings
When someone changes department or job, their access should change with them. Enter the user name: the platform reads the account's current groups from Active Directory. Tick the groups they leave and the groups the new job needs to join (only from the administrator's allowed list, never an admin group). The receiving manager approves the access, then an administrator who is neither the requester nor that manager approves the change. On the effective date at 06:00 Riyadh time the platform removes the old groups first, adds the new ones, updates department and job title, and reads the account back to confirm. Privileged accounts and admin groups are never changed here - a domain administrator does that.
