Processor Management

Every party that processes personal data for {{org.short}} (TPAs, reinsurers, call centres, cloud and IT services, printing, collection agencies) works only on written instructions and must give sufficient guarantees. The agreement carries the PDPL terms; the processor is assessed periodically (here: every 12 months, due-diligence questionnaire scored in Python); each sub-processor is approved in advance by the DPO or an administrator who did not propose it. A processor that breaks the agreement becomes a controller and is liable itself (PDPL Art. 8; Implementing Regulation Art. 17).
