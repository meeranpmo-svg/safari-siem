Start an NDMO Gap Assessment

A step-by-step gap assessment against the NDMO Data Management and Personal Data Protection Standards: name the team and the scope, request the evidence from each department across the 15 data management domains, take a baseline of the NDMO register (maturity per domain), complete the control review (every control passed by the platform or recorded by a person and approved by the Chief Data Officer), rate every gap, publish the remediation plan to the GRC actions and sign and approve the report. Every step shows what is done and what blocks it; a named person decides every step and every action is in the audit trail. An internal assessment, not the regulator's assessment.
