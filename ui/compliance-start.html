Compliance Programme

How {{org.short}} starts, achieves and keeps compliance with each regulation, step by step, on the platform's own compliance registers. The platform measures what it can from its records; a control it cannot measure needs evidence a person records; a second person independently approves it; an automated gap always wins over a confirmation, so no gap can be closed on paper. Gaps become tracked remediation actions. This is an internal self-assessment, not a regulator's certificate.
