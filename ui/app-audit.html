Application Audit

One audit report per application (the servers grouped as one business service in All Applications): availability and resilience, every change made to its servers with who proposed, approved and applied it, logon and account activity on its servers from the SIEM, and whether each server is sending logs at all - a server that sent nothing is a finding, never "nothing happened". Its database activity is in Databases → Database Audit.
