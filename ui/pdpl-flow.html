PDPL Automation Flow

From the personal data the platform finds to obligations that are verified closed: read-only discovery finds personal data, departments record their processing, the DPO signs impact and transfer assessments and processors are reviewed, consent is recorded, requests run on the 30-day clock and breaches on the 72-hour clock, the control monitor tests the systems, and the Privacy Office routes every gap to a named person - closed only when the registers no longer show it.
