Personal Data Protection (PDPL) · Saudi PDPL (SDAIA)

AI PDPL Consultant

The consultant oversees compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law: it checks the registers against 24 requirements, raises every gap as work for Governance, and prepares the compliance statement. It changes nothing and signs nothing. The statement is signed by the appointed DPO and countersigned by a different person in management. It is an internal self-assessment, not an SDAIA certificate: only SDAIA, or a body it accredits, can certify.

Personal data breaches

From the moment a person decides an incident affects personal data, SDAIA must be told within 72 hours (Implementing Regulation Art. 24). The platform prepares the SDAIA notification and the data subject notice; the DPO files them in SDAIA's portal - nothing is sent from here.
