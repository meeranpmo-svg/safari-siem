Audit Automation Flow

From the platform's own records to findings that are closed after a re-test: use cases test every record, continuous control monitoring tests the audit trail, analytics test the datasets, findings are proposed for an auditor to accept into an engagement, the owner answers with an action plan, an auditor re-tests, and the finding closes.
