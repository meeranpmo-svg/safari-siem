PDPL Governance

A PDPL programme needs clear decision-making authority, not only policy documents: a DPO with direct access to executive management and the authority to stop risky processing; a named person (not a job title) for each function; a data protection committee of legal, IT, HR and operations meeting at least quarterly; a privacy review before any new system or product goes live; and a yearly programme review whose findings become actions with an owner and a due date, closed with evidence.
