Support contracts

Contracts

Each contract covers a family (all Windows servers, all ESXi hosts…) or listed assets / serials. Checked against the inventory: dates, what is not covered, quantity, retired or unknown devices. Vendor confirmation: Cisco is checked with Cisco; for the others an engineer records what was checked, per term.

CSV columns: vendor, family, product, contract, level, start, end, quantity, unit, serials, assets, owner, cost, currency, notes

Assets no active contract covers

Recognised from facts in the asset record (OS, installed software, tags, vendor / model) — the reason is shown. Add the contract, or list these assets on an existing one.

Support and subscriptions reported by the firewalls

FortiGate and Palo Alto firewalls report their own support contract and security subscriptions (antivirus, IPS, URL filtering, sandbox…). The AI Network Engineer reads them every 6 hours over its read-only API connection; expired or ending items appear as problems and SIEM alerts. An expired subscription means that protection is no longer updated.
