Solvency & Capital

The Insurance Authority's Solvency and Capital template: the results Excel calculated, the template's checks, sign-off by every area, four-eyes approval by the Risk Head - and the same workbook back for the Authority.
Business Risk

Collect from the departments

Each department downloads the template, fills its own sheets in SR '000 and uploads it; only its sheets are read. Every value is checked against last year and the history of approved returns (units x1,000, sign flips, blanks, unusual changes); a flagged value needs the department's explanation, and a different person in the Risk function accepts each pack. The AI Business Risk Officer (Business Risk dashboard) can report progress and draft explanations - a person writes them in.

Returns

The template's formulas stay the calculation: the platform reads the values Excel computed when the file was saved, and never changes a formula. A newer upload for the same date replaces a draft; after approval it is a resubmission. Amounts are SR '000, as in the template.
