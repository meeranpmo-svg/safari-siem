How our automation works

Data sourcesAI engineer does itA named person decidesIndependent AI audit
1
Read
Devices, servers, databases, applications, the SIEM and the asset register — read-only accounts.
2
Patrol
Every 10 min each AI engineer checks its area and opens one work item per problem.
3
Investigate
Read-only checks and evidence; the likely cause and the recommended fix are written on the item.
4
Propose
A change plan or patch request is recorded. Nothing is executed.
5
Approve
A named human approver decides — never the requester, never a service or AI account.
6
Apply
A person carries it out (or the tool, only after that approval).
7
Verify
The AI engineer re-checks: fixed → resolved; still there → back to a person.
8
Audit
The AI IT and Cyber Security Auditors test the controls — including steps 4–6 — and send findings to the owning department.
