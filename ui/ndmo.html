NDMO Data Management

The 15 domains of the NDMO Data Management and Personal Data Protection Standards, as a register for {{org.short}}. The platform checks each control against what it already holds - the PDPL registers, the Data Catalog, the GRC policy register, database monitoring, backups and route maps - and re-checks on every patrol of the AI Data Governance Officer. The Personal Data Protection domain is the PDPL register itself, so the two can never disagree. A person records the rest with evidence and a second person approves (the Chief Data Officer or an administrator). Register controls (ZS-…) are this platform's plain-language register; the official NDMO controls are imported from the NDMO document and mapped to them. The maturity level is indicative, from this register - it is not NDMO's own assessment.
