Data Subject Requests

Requests from customers and employees to access, copy, correct or destroy their personal data, withdraw consent or object. Staff record each request here when it arrives (branch, call centre, e-mail, web, letter). Only the request reference is kept here: the person's name and details stay in the customer systems. The answer is due in 30 days; once, with notice and reasons before the 30 days end, it may be extended by 30 days. Identity is verified first, every system that holds the person's data is worked, and the answer letter (Arabic and English) is drafted from the request.
