Cross-Border Transfers

Personal data leaves the Kingdom only for a permitted purpose (PDPL Art. 29) and on a basis: an SDAIA adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, binding common rules, certification, code of conduct) or an exemption. Safeguards, exemptions and continuous or large-scale sensitive transfers need a transfer impact assessment first (Cross-Border Transfer Regulation Art. 8): purpose, scope, safeguards, minimisation, impact on people and the measures that reduce it. A transfer must stop at once if the assessment shows high risk or the safeguards no longer hold. The assessment is scored in Python and signed by the DPO, who did not prepare it.
