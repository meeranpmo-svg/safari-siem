Access Reviews

Every quarter the platform reads the members of the Active Directory groups in scope (read-only) and asks each member's line manager (the AD "manager" field) whether the person still needs that access. Nobody reviews their own access: without a manager the group's fallback reviewer does. Mark each line keep or remove and sign the attestation. Removals are approved by an administrator who did not ask for them, then removed in the directory and read back; privileged groups are removed by hand by a domain administrator. The export is the audit evidence (SAMA CSF 3.3.5, NCA ECC 2-2-1-5).
