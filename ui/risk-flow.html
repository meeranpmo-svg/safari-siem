Enterprise Risk Automation Flow

From the department's workbooks and the platform's own signals to decisions the risk committee can stand behind: the methodology rates every risk on the server, machine learning reads the register and the appetite metrics, the AI Risk Officer turns what needs doing into proposals, a supervisor checks each one against the records, a named person decides, and the decision becomes an action - and teaches the officer.
