The five obligations at a glance

🛡️

NCA Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC)

National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA)
What
The national minimum cybersecurity controls in five domains: governance, defence, resilience, third-party & cloud, and industrial control systems.
Applies to
Government entities and organisations that own, operate or host critical national infrastructure, and their service providers.
Our scope
The operating company that serves covered entities.
If we do not: findings in NCA assessments, and clients that must comply cannot keep a non-compliant provider.
🔐

Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA)
What
How personal data of individuals in the Kingdom is collected, used, kept, shared and transferred abroad; data-subject rights; breach notification.
Applies to
Every organisation processing personal data in the Kingdom - employees, customers, visitors.
Our scope
Every company of the group.
If we do not: fines up to SAR 5 million per violation (doubled for a repeat); disclosing sensitive data unlawfully can mean up to 2 years in prison and up to SAR 3 million.
🗂️

NDMO data management standards

National Data Management Office (SDAIA)
What
National standards for data governance, classification, quality, sharing and the protection of data - the operating model behind PDPL.
Applies to
Government entities, and the organisations that create or handle government data for them.
Our scope
Data we handle on government and national projects; good practice for the whole group.
If we do not: data we hold for clients cannot be classified, shared or audited the way their contracts require.
🌐

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

International standard - certified by an accredited body
What
An information security management system: risk assessment, 93 controls in four themes (organisational, people, physical, technological), continual improvement.
Applies to
Voluntary - but asked for in tenders and by international partners as proof of mature security.
Our scope
The certifiable backbone that NCA, PDPL and Aramco evidence can sit on.
If we do not: tenders scored on certified security are lost to competitors who hold it.
🛢️

Aramco Cybersecurity Compliance Certificate (CCC)

Saudi Aramco Third-Party Cybersecurity Standard
What
Aramco's cybersecurity requirements for contractors and suppliers, verified by an approved audit firm and certified.
Applies to
Any third party that delivers services or connects to Aramco - the controls depend on the type of service.
Our scope
Every contract with Aramco, today and in tenders.
If we do not: without a valid certificate we cannot be awarded or renew Aramco work.

A pragmatic path

First 90 days
  • Name the executive sponsor and owners
  • Gap assessment against NCA ECC and PDPL
  • Register of personal data (RoPA) and data classification
  • Fix critical gaps: MFA, backups, logging
3 - 6 months
  • Policies approved and published
  • Vendor and third-party risk process
  • Incident response and breach notification drill
  • Aramco CCC readiness for the service class
6 - 12 months
  • Aramco CCC audit and certificate
  • ISO 27001 internal audit and certification
  • Continuous control monitoring on the platform
  • Board reporting each quarter

What we ask of the leadership

  1. Sponsor the programme and name one accountable executive.
  2. Approve the owners for each framework and the budget for the first year.
  3. Review the compliance position every quarter on the platform's CISO and GRC dashboards.
