AI IT & Cyber Security Auditors

The auditors are independent of the people and AI engineers they audit: they read records, test controls and report — they change nothing and approve nothing. A test passes only on evidence; a source that is not connected is not tested, never passed, and its earlier findings stay open. Each finding goes to the owning department; it closes when the auditor's re-test passes (or a person records a risk acceptance with a reason).
1 · Test
every patrol, each control in the programme
2 · Find
an exception becomes a finding with evidence and criteria
3 · Route
to the department that owns the fix
4 · Re-test
fixed → closed; still there → stays open
