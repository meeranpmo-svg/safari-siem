Services, high availability and DR

Servers grouped by service (set by an administrator, or guessed from names such as …db1 / …db2 — marked "guessed"). A production service on one server is a single point of failure; a production service with no member at the DR site has no DR copy. UAT / test / dev / "np" servers are not expected to have HA or DR unless you mark them as required.

Sites, DR and restart policy

Map subnets to sites and mark the DR site. "Days without restart" flags servers that have not been restarted (patched) within the limit.
