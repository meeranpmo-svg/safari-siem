Department Checklist

What each department must keep doing - every month, quarter, half or year - to stay on SAMA CSF, NCA ECC, PDPL, NDMO and the Insurance Authority rules. The department head attests each obligation with the evidence reference (the evidence stays in the document store); an auditor who is not from that department verifies it. Fully compliant means done and verified; an attestation not yet verified is not counted as compliant; a period that ends without an attestation becomes overdue after the grace days.
