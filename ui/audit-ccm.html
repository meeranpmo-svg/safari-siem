Continuous Control Monitoring

Every record of the platform's hash-chained audit trail for the last 30 days, tested every day against eight controls - the whole population, not a sample. The statistics (hour-of-day rarity, robust z-scores against the person's own history and their peers) flag what is unusual: they are indicators for an auditor to follow up, not proof of wrongdoing. A test with nothing to test reads not testable, never effective. An auditor explains each exception or drafts a finding; a finding exists only when an auditor on the engagement accepts it.
