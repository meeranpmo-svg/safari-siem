Role Workflows

The recurring work of each IT role as a guided checklist. Each step shows what the platform itself records right now - servers not seen, end of life, the AI engineers' open findings, overdue GRC work. A step the platform cannot measure says "confirm" and is never shown as fine. You sign each step off (done, issue or not applicable, with a note when it needs one), and hand the period over to the next person.
