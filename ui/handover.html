Staff Handover

When an IT or cyber security employee leaves: everything they are responsible for, who takes it over, the access to remove, and their knowledge notes - signed off by their manager
Open a handover for the person leaving. The platform lists what is named after them (assets they own or hold, business services, risks, actions, support contracts, domains, their job role, plans they asked for, credentials they saved). Name a successor for each item - the successor accepts it themselves. Someone other than the leaver closes each access item with evidence. The leaver writes the notes (where secrets are kept - never the secrets). The manager signs off when everything is closed; only then do the owner fields move to the successors. The platform never disables an account or changes a password itself.
