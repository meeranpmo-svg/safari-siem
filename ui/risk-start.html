Start a Risk Assessment

A step-by-step path through a risk and control self-assessment (RCSA) on the Business Risk register: add new risks with machine-learning suggestions, open the cycle, re-assess every risk with your own methodology (likelihood × impact, exposure after controls → Class I-IV), decide the treatment of Class III / IV risks, have each department signed off by someone who did not assess it, and have the risk committee approve the cycle. The ratings are calculated on the server, never typed; every step is in the audit trail.
