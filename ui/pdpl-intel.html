PDPL Intelligence

Three pieces of a privacy analyst's work, done on the PDPL registers: a column model that learns from your reviews which discovered columns really hold personal data (and asks you about the ones it is least sure of first), the deadline risk of every open data subject request (30 days, Art. 4) and breach (72 hours to SDAIA, IR Art. 24) from this organisation's own history, and impact assessment drafts for activities that need one (IR Art. 25) - approved by the DPO. Every figure says how it was made. It ranks the work; people decide.
