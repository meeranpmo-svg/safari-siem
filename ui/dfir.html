Digital Forensics & Incident Response

The forensic side of each SOC case: when the company became aware, the regulatory clocks that started (PDPL 72 hours by law; SAMA, NCA and the Insurance Authority from the hours an administrator enters), the evidence register with a hash-chained chain of custody, indicators with their sightings, and a reconstruction of any host, user or IP from the SIEM's events. Machine learning finds bursts, attack stages in order, first-seen values and pivots - indicators, not proof. Nothing here touches an endpoint or contacts a regulator: people do, and the platform records it.
