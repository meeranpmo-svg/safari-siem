Data Protection Impact Assessment

An impact assessment is required before processing sensitive data, combining data from two or more sources, continuous monitoring, solely automated decisions, or anything else likely to harm a person's privacy (PDPL Art. 22; Implementing Regulation Art. 25). The questionnaire is scored in Python (likelihood + severity, each measure reduces it, every point named). The result is a draft until the DPO signs it, and the DPO cannot sign an assessment they prepared. Re-assess when the activity changes, at least every 2 years.
