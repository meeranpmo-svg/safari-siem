SAMA Cyber Security Framework & Insurance Authority — automated assessment

Every SAMA CSF requirement is checked every day against its own evidence: GRC (policies, risk, attestations, control monitoring, audit findings), Hardening (baseline coverage, critical failures, patching), Pentest / VAPT (scans, test cadence, exposed services) and SIEM logs (log sources, monitoring, retention). A source that is not connected reads not measured, never a pass; an automated gap is never hidden by a confirmation. What needs a document is recorded by a person and approved by the CISO. Maturity levels and scores are indicative, from this register — not SAMA's or an auditor's rating.
