The records of processing activities (Implementing Regulation Art. 33) are collected department by department. Each department's data steward describes what the department does with personal data - starting from the processing an insurer's department typically runs and from the systems the Data Catalog found holding personal data in the department. Every activity is checked as it is written; the DPO, or an administrator, who did not submit it approves it into the register.

Workflow automation machine learning over this campaign - forecasts, risks and proposals; nothing is saved until a person accepts it
