Audit · Reports

Audit Reports drafted from the engagement's own records

The platform assembles each report from the engagement's records - scope, period, objectives, procedures performed, samples and results, every finding with management's response, and the overall rating by a stated rule. The AI can only propose an executive summary and narrative; it is in the report when an auditor saves it as their own edit. People decide: the engagement lead signs as author, an administrator who is not on the audit team approves it for issue, and the issued report is frozen with its SHA-256 fingerprint. Reports name people and weaknesses, so they are for operators and administrators only.
