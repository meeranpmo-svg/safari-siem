Sensitive Data Controls

Sensitive data (health, genetic, biometric, criminal, religious, ethnic origin) and credit data carry stricter rules: explicit consent or another documented basis, access by named roles only, encryption, masking outside production, every access logged, and an impact assessment. Health data follows the Council of Health Insurance / MoH controls with every processing stage documented; credit data needs consent and the person told of every disclosure request. Each activity in the records of processing that holds such data is listed with what is in place and what is missing.
