When a playbook is applied to a case, its containment steps (block an IP, disable an account, revoke sessions…) become approval requests with the targets taken from the case's alerts. Approving records the decision in the case timeline and the audit log; the SIEM does not change the firewall, Active Directory or Microsoft 365 itself - the responder carries out the approved action and ticks the task in the case.
