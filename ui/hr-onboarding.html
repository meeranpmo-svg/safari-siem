HR Onboarding

Departments
HR records the new joiner, the line manager approves, the desktop team reserves a laptop from stock and proposes the account, and an administrator who did not propose it approves the account. When an administrator has set up Directory settings, the platform then creates the account in Active Directory itself - disabled, with a random temporary password that must be changed at first sign-in, and only in allowed, non-admin groups. The line manager sees that password once and hands it to the joiner; it is never logged, e-mailed or shown again, and the account is enabled when the joiner signs for the laptop. Without Directory settings, the person who creates the account by hand records it here and the platform never holds a password. The laptop becomes "in use" in the asset register when the joiner signs for it. Admin groups are never granted through onboarding.
