AI Engineering Team

AI engineers on duty around the clock, across security operations, the FortiGate and H3C network, NAC, Windows and Hyper-V servers, Azure, identity and PAM, Mimecast and Microsoft 365 email, compliance (NCA ECC, PDPL, NDMO, ISO 27001, Aramco CCC), audit and desktop support. Each one patrols its area, opens a work item for every problem, investigates it read-only, proposes the fix and verifies it after a person applies it. None of them changes anything on its own: change plans and patch installs are approved by an administrator (never the requester) and applied by a person.

How a work item moves

1
Patrol — every 10 minutes the engineer reads its area (devices, servers, firewalls, attack surface, desktops).
2
Open — each problem is one work item; one that comes back after a fix is re-opened with its history.
3
Investigate — read-only checks and the troubleshooter; serious items also get an AI root-cause analysis.
4
Propose — a change plan (or a patch install request) recorded for approval. Nothing is executed.
5
Approve & apply — an administrator approves, a person applies, as before.
6
Verify — the engineer re-checks the target; fixed → resolved, still there → back to a person.
