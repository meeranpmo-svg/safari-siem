Regulator Evidence Packs

Every month the platform builds a frozen evidence pack per framework - SAMA CSF, Insurance Authority, NCA ECC, PDPL and NDMO - from the registers as they stand, with a SHA-256 fingerprint, so an auditor's request is answered from a pack that already exists. Each control shows its result as the register holds it, the evidence the platform has (record counts, test runs, policies, evidence files' names and hashes - never the files themselves), the owner and what is missing. A compliance reviewer (an administrator who did not build the pack) signs it off; a signed pack cannot change. The last 24 packs per framework are kept.
