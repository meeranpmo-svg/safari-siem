Breach Response

From the moment a person decides an incident affects personal data, SDAIA must be told within 72 hours, and the people affected without undue delay when harm is likely (Implementing Regulation Art. 24). Each breach follows the playbook: contain, assess (what data, how many people, sensitive?, likely harm), notify SDAIA, notify the data subjects, remediate, lessons learned. Every step is stamped with who and when. The platform prepares the SDAIA notification and the data-subject notice; the DPO files them in SDAIA's portal - nothing is sent from here. Each breach also carries a decision for the other regulators (Insurance Authority, SAMA, NCA, Council of Health Insurance): required or not, and when and under which reference each was told.
