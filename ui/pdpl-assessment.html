An internal self-assessment against the Personal Data Protection Law, its Implementing Regulation and the Cross-Border Transfer Regulation. Answers need evidence; approval is by the DPO or an administrator who did not submit it. Approval writes the results to the PDPL requirements and sends every gap to the GRC register. It is not an SDAIA certificate.
