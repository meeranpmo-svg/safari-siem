PDPL Programme

PDPL compliance is a business capability, not a legal checkbox: legal interprets, governance sets standards and ownership, IT builds the controls, marketing handles opt-ins and product builds privacy in by design. Each department names a data steward - the bridge between the compliance goals and its day-to-day work - and keeps short team playbooks. The obligations below are a controller's duties under the law and its Implementing Regulation, each with what the registers show and the penalty it carries. The maturity assessment scores four areas: data mapping and ownership, consent, team responsibilities and training, documentation and audit readiness.
