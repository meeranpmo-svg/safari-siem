Asset Ownership

Every asset needs an IT owner (who runs and fixes it) and a business owner (who is accountable for the service and its data)
Set owners per business service once and every asset of that service inherits them; an owner set on the asset itself always wins. Owners are never guessed from a department or a name. Every change is recorded with who, when and why (ISO 27001 A.5.9 · SAMA CSF asset management · NCA ECC 2-1).
