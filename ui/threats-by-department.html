How alerts are matched to a department: the alert's account (or target account) → its Department in Active Directory; if the alert has only a computer, the account that last signed in to it interactively (last 14 days). Nothing is guessed — what cannot be matched is listed as Not attributed with the reason. Risk = info 0 · low 1 · medium 3 · high 7 · critical 15 per alert; per 100 users divides by the department's enabled AD accounts so a large department does not look worse only because it is large.
